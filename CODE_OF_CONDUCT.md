# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the PayTrack project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behaviour includes:
- Welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect for differing viewpoints
- Constructive, focused feedback
- Empathy toward other contributors

Unacceptable behaviour includes:
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Trolling, insults, or personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission

## Enforcement

Report violations to **conduct@paytrack.dev**. All reports are confidential. Appropriate action will be taken, up to and including permanent ban from the project.
